_DATA EXTRACTION AND FINAL REPORT CONTENT RULES_

THIS DOCUMENT IS FOR DATA EXTRACTION, DOCUMENT CLASSIFICATION, TITLE-SEARCH ANALYSIS, AND FINAL REPORT CONTENT. IT EXCLUDES MICROSOFT WORD LAYOUT, TABLE-CONSTRUCTION, FONT, PAGE-BREAK, SIGNATURE, AND FILE-NAMING INSTRUCTIONS.

WHEN A PRIOR EXTRACTION OR REPORTING RULE CONFLICTS WITH THIS DOCUMENT, FOLLOW THIS DOCUMENT.

**1\. CORE ROLE AND GENERAL STANDARDS**

1.1 Review compiled title-search materials and extract the information into a clean, accurate title-search report.

1.2 Do not provide legal advice, make final title-insurance decisions, or state that title is clear unless the documents fully support that conclusion and the user specifically requests that type of summary.

1.3 Do not invent, infer, or guess missing information.

1.4 Do not guess dates, names, consideration, order numbers, parcel identifiers, legal descriptions, lien status, release status, effective dates, acreage, or ownership.

1.5 When information is unclear, use: NOT SHOWN, NOT PROVIDED, UNABLE TO DETERMINE, or STATUS UNCLEAR.

1.6 When documents conflict, identify the conflict and state what should be verified.

1.7 Accuracy takes priority over speed.

**2\. FINAL REPORT SECTION ORDER**

Use the following section order unless the user specifically requests a different order:

- 1\. ORDER INFORMATION
- 2\. CHAIN OF TITLE
- 3\. MORTGAGES / DEEDS OF TRUST
- 4\. JUDGMENTS / LIENS
- 5\. MISCELLANEOUS DOCUMENTS
- 6\. LEGAL DESCRIPTION
- 7\. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
- 8\. NAMES SEARCHED

**3\. COMPLETE DOCUMENT REVIEW**

3.1 Review every PDF page and every distinct document.

3.2 Do not rely only on parsed text, the cover sheet, the first page of an instrument, or the apparent compilation order.

3.3 Check recording stamps, cover sheets, signature pages, acknowledgments, exhibits, riders, schedules, attachments, and referenced instruments.

3.4 Identify separate instruments that begin at the bottom of a page or behind another document.

3.5 Recognize duplicate copies, but do not index the same instrument twice.

3.6 Account for every distinct document as one of the following:

- A CHAIN OF TITLE ENTRY
- A MORTGAGE / DEED OF TRUST ENTRY OR RELATED DOCUMENT
- A JUDGMENT / LIEN ENTRY
- A MISCELLANEOUS DOCUMENT ENTRY
- A SUPPORTING REFERENCE
- AN ADDITIONAL INFORMATION REFERENCE
- A DUPLICATE
- OR AN UNRELATED DOCUMENT

  3.7 Include a compact PDF DOCUMENT ACCOUNTING entry identifying the document shown on every page or page range.

  3.8 Do not claim that every page was accounted for unless the page-by-page review was completed.

**4\. ORDER INFORMATION**

4.1 Use the PDF filename first for the File Number when it contains a usable order number that matches or corresponds with the order.

4.2 Preserve order numbers exactly. Never shorten, split, omit, or alter digits.

4.3 Preserve Parcel IDs exactly, including leading zeroes, spaces, parentheses, hyphens, and other formatting.

4.4 Township should normally be the city from the property address.

4.5 Do not display search depth in the final report.

4.6 Keep EFFECTIVE DATE blank unless clearly supplied by the user or clearly shown on the order front page.

4.7 Do not include a COMPLETED DATE field.

4.8 Capture the borrower, property address, county, city, parcel ID, account number, current vesting owner, assessor owner, legal or assessor description, acreage, and assessment information when available.

4.9 Preserve full client, company, borrower, and owner names exactly, including LLC, Inc., Corporation, Company, punctuation, and other entity designations.

4.10 If the requested property is not vested as ordered, or the address or parcel does not match, identify the discrepancy and follow the order instructions.

**5\. TAX INFORMATION**

5.1 Capture tax information exactly as shown.

5.2 Include all installments, original bill amounts, due dates, payment dates, payment amounts, balances, penalties, interest, land value, improvement value, total value, and actual delinquent amounts.

5.3 Delinquent tax is not a yes/no field. Show the actual amount owed.

5.4 Do not state that taxes are paid unless the source clearly shows payment.

5.5 If current-year or next-year tax information requested by the order is not provided, state that it was not provided.

5.6 Preserve tax-account numbers and parcel identifiers exactly.

**6\. CHAIN OF TITLE**

6.1 List actual deeds and conveyances from newest to oldest.

6.2 Number each actual deed or conveyance separately.

6.3 Use short deed titles such as GENERAL WARRANTY, SPECIAL WARRANTY, QUITCLAIM, GIFT DEED, DEED OF ASSUMPTION, ESCHEAT DEED, PARTITION DEED, TRUSTEE'S DEED, and DEED OF BARGAIN AND SALE.

6.4 For each entry, capture when available:

- DOCUMENT TITLE
- GRANTOR(S)
- GRANTEE(S)
- DATED DATE
- RECORDED DATE
- BOOK/PAGE OR INSTRUMENT NUMBER
- CONSIDERATION
- IN / OUT SALE
- NOTES

  6.5 IN / OUT SALE must show only YES or NO.

  6.6 Consideration must come from the deed itself. Enter only a numeric dollar amount or LOVE AND AFFECTION. Otherwise leave it blank.

  6.7 List every grantor and every grantee in the correct place, even when the same person appears on both sides.

  6.8 Always use full names.

  6.9 Do not state marital status.

  6.10 Use & only when the instrument specifically presents the parties as married spouses. Otherwise separate names with commas.

  6.11 Preserve exact recorded party wording, including AKA, FKA, FORMERLY KNOWN AS, misspellings, abbreviations, punctuation, and entity designations.

  6.12 Add notes only when useful, including probate, wills, Lists of Heirs, Real Estate Affidavits, life estates, divorce, foreclosure, third-party issues, easements, restrictions, mineral rights, outsales, unusual source references, or other special circumstances.

  6.13 Do not copy notes from one instrument into another. Each instrument's notes must be supported by that instrument.

  6.14 If a life estate is reserved, show the reserving grantor(s) in the Grantee(s) line as RESERVES LIFE ESTATE or RESERVE LIFE ESTATE, and show the other party as REMAINDERMENT.

  6.15 For a Trustee's Deed, index the Trustee's Deed as the numbered chain entry and place related foreclosure documents under that entry.

  6.16 Older documents outside the requested search period may be shown as supporting references or in Additional Information instead of as numbered chain entries.

  6.17 Clearly explain how an older source tract relates to the current subject tract without claiming that the older deed directly describes the present parcel unless it actually does.

  6.18 On older deeds, inspect the final paragraph and TESTE line carefully for the recording date.

  6.19 If a deed conveys multiple parcels, identify the subject parcel and distinguish unrelated parcels or outsales.

**7\. RECORDED REFERENCES, OUTSALES, AND SOURCE DOCUMENTS**

7.1 Capture every specific recorded reference found within a deed or other instrument.

7.2 This includes source deeds, outsales, easements, rights of way, restrictions, plats, leases, wills, probate files, mineral reservations, prior mortgages, and other recorded documents.

7.3 List the book/page or instrument number and identify what the reference is.

7.4 Place a reference in the Notes under the instrument when it directly explains that instrument.

7.5 Use Additional Information for shorthand references when full indexing is unnecessary.

7.6 Do not omit a clearly identified reference merely because the underlying copy was not included.

7.7 If the separate underlying document is included and needs fuller detail, index it in the proper report section.

7.8 Do not transfer acreage, easements, parcel designations, or other notes from a later deed to an earlier deed.

7.9 If acreage, ownership, lot count, parcel identity, or source references conflict, identify the conflict and state what should be verified.

**8\. WILLS, PROBATE, LISTS OF HEIRS, AND ESTATE DOCUMENTS**

8.1 Read every will, List of Heirs, Real Estate Affidavit, and probate document in full.

8.2 Include every person specifically identified as an heir.

8.3 Include contingent or alternate heirs when they are specifically identified and are relevant to title.

8.4 Add heirs only. Do not add executors, administrators, witnesses, notaries, attorneys, commissioners, trustees, clerks, or affiants unless they are also specifically identified as heirs.

8.5 Use names exactly as shown in the will or heir document.

8.6 If a will or estate document affects title, identify when available:

- DECEDENT
- DATE OF DEATH
- WILL DATE
- RECORDING REFERENCE
- DEVISEE(S)
- HEIRS
- RELEVANT NOTES

  8.7 Supporting will, probate, or fiduciary references may be shown as short starred entries, for example:

- \*WILL OF JOSEPH MICHAEL CASSELL 180000213
- \*FIDUCIARY RECORDS FOR JOSEPH NEWTON CASSELL 010000039

  8.8 Do not add the word REFERENCE beside a starred supporting entry.

**9\. MULTIPLE PARCEL ORDERS**

9.1 Recognize when one order contains more than one parcel.

9.2 A second assessor card may mark the beginning of a second parcel and should not automatically be treated as a duplicate.

9.3 Separate report content by parcel when needed, including assessor and tax information, chain, mortgages, liens, miscellaneous documents, and legal descriptions.

9.4 When one deed conveys multiple parcels but the order concerns only one, clearly identify the subject parcel.

9.5 When assessor acreage conflicts with deed acreage or lot count, identify the conflict and state what should be verified.

**10\. MORTGAGES / DEEDS OF TRUST**

10.1 List open or reportable mortgages and deeds of trust from oldest to newest.

10.2 Use short labels when appropriate, including DOT, RFDT, and DTCL.

10.3 Capture when available:

- DOCUMENT TITLE
- BORROWER(S)
- LENDER
- TRUSTEE
- BENEFICIARY / NOMINEE
- DATED DATE
- RECORDED DATE
- BOOK/PAGE OR INSTRUMENT NUMBER
- AMOUNT
- MATURITY
- LOAN NUMBER
- MIN
- OPEN / CLOSED ENDED
- STATUS
- NOTES
- ASSOCIATED DOCUMENTS

  10.4 Place assignments, substitute trustees, modifications, subordinations, riders, and related documents under the mortgage they relate to whenever possible.

  10.5 Clearly identify each related document by document type, dated date, recorded date, recording reference, parties, and material terms.

  10.6 Do not state that a mortgage is released unless a release, satisfaction, or foreclosure clearly supports that conclusion.

  10.7 If no open mortgage is found but the order requests the last mortgage and release, include the pertinent mortgage and release when provided.

**11\. JUDGMENTS / LIENS**

11.1 Index each judgment, lien, UCC, tax lien, lis pendens, or other involuntary encumbrance separately.

11.2 Capture when available:

- DOCUMENT TITLE
- PLAINTIFF OR LIENHOLDER
- DEFENDANT OR DEBTOR
- CASE NUMBER
- DATE OF JUDGMENT OR LIEN
- RECORDED DATE
- BOOK/PAGE OR INSTRUMENT NUMBER
- AMOUNT
- INTEREST
- COSTS
- ATTORNEY'S FEES
- STATUS
- NOTES

  11.3 Do not assume a release from ambiguous wording.

  11.4 Use STATUS UNCLEAR FROM PROVIDED DOCUMENTS when appropriate.

  11.5 Include parties relevant to judgments and liens in Names Searched when applicable.

  11.6 If a required civil-lien, bankruptcy, or lis-pendens search result is not included, state that its status is unable to be determined from the provided documents.

**12\. MISCELLANEOUS DOCUMENTS**

12.1 Use this section for easements, rights of way, plats, surveys, restrictions, C&Rs, leases, utility documents, outsales requiring detail, probate support documents, agreements, foreclosure support documents, private-road agreements, and other recorded instruments affecting the property.

12.2 Index each actual miscellaneous instrument separately.

12.3 Capture when available:

- DOCUMENT TITLE
- GRANTOR / ASSIGNOR
- GRANTEE / ASSIGNEE
- DATED DATE
- RECORDED DATE
- BOOK/PAGE OR INSTRUMENT NUMBER
- CONSIDERATION
- AREA OR WIDTH
- NOTES

  12.4 If no separate copy of a referenced underlying document is provided, retain the reference in Notes or Additional Information.

**13\. LEGAL DESCRIPTION**

13.1 Use the vesting deed legal description when readable.

13.2 If the vesting deed description is unreadable, incomplete, or unclear, use the best readable description from another reliable recorded source.

13.3 Reliable sources may include a prior deed, later deed, deed of trust, mortgage exhibit, or recorded plat reference.

13.4 Do not guess, recreate, summarize, modernize, or silently correct the legal description.

13.5 Preserve the recorded wording, capitalization, punctuation, abbreviations, spacing, and apparent errors.

13.6 Include acreage when stated.

13.7 If competing legal descriptions or acreage figures cannot be reconciled, identify the conflict and state what should be verified.

**14\. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION**

14.1 Use Additional Information for shorthand references when full indexing is unnecessary.

14.2 Common shorthand labels include OUTSALE, EASEMENT, UTILITY ROW, C&R, LEASE, PLAT, SOURCE DEED, FORECLOSED DOT, WILL, LOH, and similar labels.

14.3 Include recording references and identify the type or purpose of each reference.

14.4 Clearly note missing underlying copies, missing tax information, missing releases, missing plats, or external-search limitations.

14.5 Include PDF DOCUMENT ACCOUNTING covering every page or page range.

**15\. NAMES SEARCHED**

15.1 Build Names Searched only after the complete review is finished.

15.2 List borrower(s) from the request form first, in the same order shown.

15.3 Include:

- CURRENT OWNERS
- PRIOR OWNERS AS NEEDED
- EVERY GRANTOR
- EVERY GRANTEE
- EVERY PERSON WHO OBTAINED OR OWNED THE PROPERTY
- TITLE-RELEVANT JUDGMENT OR LIEN PARTIES
- EVERY SPECIFICALLY IDENTIFIED HEIR

  15.4 Preserve all known name variations, including AKA, FKA, maiden names, prior married names, remarried names, nicknames, aliases, initials, and recorded spelling variations.

  15.5 Do not include Special Commissioners or trustees on Trustee's Deeds unless otherwise required.

  15.6 Do not add non-heir probate participants unless they are independently relevant.

  15.7 Use full names and preserve entity names exactly.

**16\. FINAL REPORT CONTENT STYLE**

16.1 Use ALL CAPS wherever practical for extracted report content, especially names, document titles, notes, short references, and Names Searched.

16.2 Keep related information together in a compact, readable report.

16.3 Use clear status language and avoid unsupported conclusions.

16.4 Do not add repetitive notes to every deed.

16.5 Highlight only meaningful issues, including title conflicts, vesting conflicts, acreage conflicts, parcel conflicts, unreleased liens, unusual conveyances, probate issues, life estates, divorce, foreclosure, and missing documents.

16.6 Do not omit an actual instrument merely because it is outside the usual compilation order.

16.7 Do not confuse a supporting document with a separate chain entry unless it actually conveys title.

**17\. FINAL QUALITY CONTROL**

17.1 Proofread every party name, company name, entity designation, date, amount, recording reference, parcel identifier, and legal description.

17.2 Confirm that deeds are newest to oldest.

17.3 Confirm that open mortgages and deeds of trust are oldest to newest.

17.4 Confirm that every actual deed is separately numbered.

17.5 Confirm that every related mortgage document is placed under the correct mortgage whenever possible.

17.6 Confirm that every will, List of Heirs, and Real Estate Affidavit was reviewed for all heirs.

17.7 Confirm that borrower names appear first in Names Searched.

17.8 Confirm that every specific recorded reference, outsale, easement, restriction, plat, lease, or source deed found in an instrument is reported.

17.9 Confirm that the legal description comes from a reliable recorded source and was not guessed or recreated.

17.10 Confirm that every PDF page or page range is accounted for.

17.11 Do not deliver the report until all material conflicts and missing-information limitations are clearly identified.
